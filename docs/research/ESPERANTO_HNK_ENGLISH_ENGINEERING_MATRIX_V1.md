# SW Language Atlas — Esperanto × HNK × English Engineering Matrix V1

Status: RESEARCH BASELINE / EVIDENCE-SEPARATED

Purpose: compare a mature natural language (English), a historically successful constructed language (Esperanto), and the governed constructed/metaphysical HNK project without collapsing their evidence classes.

## Epistemic labels
- FACT_LINGUISTIC: externally documented linguistic fact.
- FACT_HISTORICAL: externally documented historical fact.
- HNK_CANON: authoritative inside HNK because defined/approved by its creator/project governance; not automatically an external linguistic fact.
- INFERENCE: analysis derived from evidence.
- HYPOTHESIS: proposition to test.
- EXPERIMENT: operational test proposed for HNK Lab.
- UNRESOLVED: insufficient approved evidence.

## Absorption labels
KEEP · ADAPT · TEST · REJECT

## Foundational contrast

### Esperanto
FACT_HISTORICAL — The Fundamento identifies three foundational works: the 16-rule grammar, Universala Vortaro, and Ekzercaro. Its preface argues for an explicitly stable foundation rather than arbitrary revision.

FACT_LINGUISTIC — The Fundamental Grammar specifies predictable reading of written words, penultimate stress, systematic grammatical endings, and productive compounding of roots. The Universala Vortaro explicitly demonstrates decomposition of complex words into separately recoverable elements.

### English
FACT_LINGUISTIC — English is a historically evolved natural language rather than a language designed from a single explicit foundational specification. For SimpleWay One it is treated as the mature product/reference language whose irregularities and conventional usage must be represented rather than normalized away by CORE.

### HNK
HNK_CANON — HNK is governed through explicit project authority and evidence/source boundaries. Existing SW-HNK work distinguishes source evidence, candidates, validated material and canon, and forbids UI/pedagogy from silently inventing missing grammar.

INFERENCE — These three systems provide complementary stress tests: English tests natural-language irregularity and conventionality; Esperanto tests deliberate regularization plus later community evolution; HNK tests language engineering under active evidence-first governance.

## High-value Esperanto mechanisms for HNK investigation

### 1. Foundation triad
Esperanto's Grammar + Vocabulary + Exercises suggests a useful engineering separation:

RULE SYSTEM + LEXICON + AUTHORITATIVE USAGE EXAMPLES

HYPOTHESIS — HNK may benefit from maintaining these as independently versioned but linked artifacts: grammar/rules, lexicon, and validated utterance corpus.

Decision: ADAPT, not copy. HNK already has richer authority states than the historical Esperanto triad.

### 2. Productive composition
Esperanto demonstrates that a compact inventory can gain expressive reach through recoverable composition.

HYPOTHESIS — HNK can measure expressive yield per learned primitive without assuming Esperanto-like morphology.

Metric candidate: Lexical Generative Power (LGP).

EXPERIMENT — Select a fully validated HNK subset; count primitives taught; measure how many novel but canonically licensed meanings/forms learners can correctly interpret or produce. No form counts unless its productive rule is independently licensed.

### 3. Predictability
Esperanto deliberately reduces decoding uncertainty through regular orthography/stress and systematic morphology.

HYPOTHESIS — Predictability should be treated as several independent dimensions rather than one simplicity score.

Candidate measures:
- grapheme→sound predictability;
- sound→grapheme predictability;
- stress predictability;
- morphological predictability;
- syntactic construction predictability;
- semantic compositional predictability.

### 4. Stable foundation + controlled evolution
Esperanto's Fundamento illustrates the social/engineering value of protecting a stable compatibility base while allowing later documented evolution.

HYPOTHESIS — HNK should distinguish compatibility stability from creative authority. The creator may retain canonical authority while releases still expose immutable versioned baselines so learners/apps do not silently change underneath a course.

Decision: ADAPT into versioned language-pack releases, not into a rule that HNK canon can never change.

### 5. Exercises as evidence-bearing material
The Ekzercaro is not merely an external workbook; it is included in the foundational package.

INFERENCE — For engineered languages, examples can help disambiguate what terse rules alone do not capture.

Decision: KEEP the HNK principle that validated utterances/surfaces carry scoped authority, while preserving the difference between example evidence and universal productive grammar.

## What English contributes that Esperanto cannot

1. Natural irregularity stress test: CORE must tolerate exceptions and non-compositional constructions.
2. Usage-driven meaning: conventional collocations and pragmatic meaning cannot always be derived from compact rules.
3. Orthographic opacity: media, pronunciation and assessment cannot assume transparent spelling.
4. Large lexical/cultural variation: Language Pack APIs must support register, dialect, polysemy and usage metadata.
5. Pedagogical reality check: a universal method must work when the target language is not engineered for learnability.

## What HNK contributes that neither comparison language supplies in the same way

1. Live language-engineering laboratory where design hypotheses can be proposed before canonization.
2. Explicit source-lock and candidate/validated boundaries.
3. Ability to test pedagogical architecture concurrently with language construction.
4. Symbolic/metaphysical internal layers that require epistemic separation from external linguistic evidence.
5. Opportunity to measure whether deliberate design choices actually improve acquisition rather than merely appearing elegant on paper.

## First experiment backlog

### EXP-01 — Predictability Ratio
Question: after learning a validated rule subset, how accurately can learners infer unseen licensed forms?
Compare: HNK subset vs matched Esperanto teaching subset vs English teaching subset.

### EXP-02 — Lexical Generative Power
Question: how many correctly interpretable/usable expressions become available per primitive learned?
Important: distinguish theoretically generable strings from forms speakers actually understand and the canon licenses.

### EXP-03 — Exception Load
Question: how many separately memorized exceptions are required to reach the same A1 capability target?

### EXP-04 — Transfer Yield
Question: can a learner apply a learned pattern correctly in a new communicative context without direct rehearsal?

### EXP-05 — Example-to-Rule Transfer
Question: when learners receive authoritative examples plus a compact rule, does transfer outperform rule-only or example-only instruction?

### EXP-06 — Governance Traceability
Question: can every taught HNK item be traced from UI → lesson surface → pack entry → authority/evidence source with zero silent invention?

## SimpleWay One architectural consequences

1. Language Pack schema needs provenance/authority hooks but must not impose the HNK authority taxonomy on every language.
2. CORE must support both regular and highly irregular languages.
3. Word formation must be pack-defined; CORE cannot assume concatenative morphology.
4. Pronunciation/stress rules are pack capabilities, not universal fields with English defaults.
5. Examples/utterances should be first-class curriculum objects with scoped authority metadata.
6. Assessment must permit pack-defined equivalence and ambiguity.
7. The Lab needs metrics for predictability, generativity, exceptions, transfer and retention.
8. Metaphysical/symbolic metadata may exist in a pack/research layer, but CORE must not treat it as empirical linguistic truth.

## Decision gate before deeper Language SDK design

KEEP current minimal `Language Pack Contract` because it remains semantically opaque.

Before expanding the SDK, test its next proposed fields against at least:
- English: natural, irregular, usage-heavy;
- Esperanto: constructed, regularized, composition-heavy;
- HNK: constructed, actively governed, evidence-first.

Later stress tests should add Toki Pona and Lojban, followed by languages with substantially different writing/morphosyntactic systems.

## Research principle
Do not ask which language is best. Ask which mechanism solves which problem, under which evidence class, at what cost, and whether the mechanism survives acquisition testing.
